How it works

1. You stay signed in to Outlook + Teams in this Chrome (your normal browsing session).

2. On a schedule (or when you click Capture now), the extension opens these four URLs in the background:

  • outlook.office.com/calendar/view/day — today's agenda
  • teams.microsoft.com/.../activity — @mentions, replies, missed calls
  • outlook.cloud.microsoft/mail/?folder=focusedinbox — focused inbox (most needs-response items live here)
  • teams.microsoft.com/.../chat — active 1:1 / group chats

3. Each tab waits ~10 seconds for content to render, the extension reads the [role="main"] inner text, then closes the tab.

4. Combined content is POSTed to your local Meeting Recorder backend; the LLM parses it into a daily briefing with check-offable action items.

5. Because the scrapes run in YOUR real Chrome (no Playwright), Microsoft's automation detection doesn't fire — your real session cookies authenticate everything.
